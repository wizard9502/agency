# Agency

**The permissionless launchpad where creators, builders, and educators launch tokens that give people real ownership, skills, and tools.**

Available on **Arc**, **BNB Chain**, **Robinhood Chain**, and **Solana**.  
Native token launching on **Arc**.

## Vision
Agency combines three powerful verticals under one roof:
- **CreatorEquity** — creators launch tokens with real ownership & revenue share
- **OpenTools Factory** — builders fund open-source tools & public goods
- **SkillForge** — educators launch tokens for skills, courses, and learning paths
